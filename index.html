Zi Wei Dou Shu – Purple Star Astrology

Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗数) is an Eastern mystical astrology from Song-dynasty China. A birth chart places over a hundred stars into twelve palaces; fourteen major stars led by the Purple Star, Zi Wei, describe temperament and life themes, while the Four Transformations show how fortune moves through each decade and year.
